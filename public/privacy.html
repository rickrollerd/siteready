SiteReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Without an account

The task and details you enter are sent to the SiteReady server to prepare a preview, and sent back. The server does not keep them. A company profile you save without an account (name, ABN, address, phone, email and logo) stays in this browser on this device.

With an account

When you sign in, SiteReady keeps, for your company:

  • your email address, name, and the email addresses of people you add to your company
  • your company profile and logo
  • the sites and SWMS you save, including the name of the person who confirmed each review
  • for each worker who signs on to a SWMS by QR code: their name, company, finger signature and the time they signed
  • if you set up Face ID or Touch ID: a public key for your device. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your device, and SiteReady never receives it.

Only people in your company can see your company's SWMS, sites and sign-ons. A worker who opens a sign-on link can see that SWMS's task, high risk work, job steps and PPE so they know what they are signing.

This is kept in a database run by our hosting provider in order to provide the service. It is not sold or used for advertising. You can ask us to delete your company's data at any time; deleted SWMS are hidden at once and removed from the database on request.

Emails

We send sign-in links, invitations from your team, and reminders when a saved SWMS is due for its 3 monthly review. We do not send marketing email without asking.

Security

Sign-in links work once and expire after 20 minutes. Sessions and links are stored only as one-way hashes. Connections to SiteReady are encrypted.

Limits on use

To limit how often the service can be used, the server keeps your IP address in memory for the length of the rate limit window, 15 minutes unless changed. It is not written to storage.

Counts and errors

To see how SiteReady is used and fix problems, the server counts actions, such as a preview, a download, a saved SWMS or a worker sign-on, with the date and the company it was for. It does not keep the content in these counts. Server errors are logged with the page and the error message.

What we don't collect

We do not collect location data, we do not use third-party analytics, and we do not use tracking cookies.

Third parties

SiteReady does not send your details to an AI provider. The server and database run on a hosting provider, and email is sent through an email provider. Payments, when you subscribe, are handled by the payment provider, which keeps your card details; SiteReady does not see them.

SiteReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What we collect

The task, company, workplace and other details you enter are sent to the SiteReady server to prepare your draft. The server prepares the draft and sends it back. It does not save what you enter.

To limit how often the service can be used, the server keeps your IP address in memory for the length of the rate limit window, 15 minutes unless changed. It is not written to storage.

What we don't collect

We do not collect location data or usage analytics, and we do not use cookies.

Third parties

SiteReady does not send your details to an AI provider or any other third party. The server runs on a hosting provider, which carries the traffic.

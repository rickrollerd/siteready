Questions

Is a SWMS from SiteWrite approved or legally checked?

No. SiteWrite drafts the SWMS. Your business checks each one against the site, changes it where needed, consults the workers and approves it, as work health and safety law requires. See the terms of use.

Does it use AI?

For some jobs, not for writing the SWMS. SiteWrite's own rules write each SWMS from the WHS laws and codes of practice for the state the project is in. The same task always gives the same draft, and each SWMS lists the laws it is based on. AI (Claude, made by Anthropic) reads a scope of works or quote you upload and suggests the work packages and job steps for you to confirm, and translates the SWMS for workers who read it in another language. The English SWMS always applies. The rules, not the AI, decide what is high risk work, which laws apply and the controls for each step. SiteWrite is updated when safety laws and codes of practice change, so your SWMS stay current.

Which states does it cover?

All states and territories.

What happens to my SWMS if I stop paying?

They stay saved. You can still preview them, and download them again once you re-subscribe. You can't save or download new SWMS until you re-subscribe.

Where is my data kept?

In a database hosted in Singapore. Your SWMS, business details and workers' details are never sold or used for advertising. We may share or sell de-identified statistics, with names, contact details, ABNs and addresses removed; the privacy policy explains this and how to opt out.

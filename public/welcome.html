Questions

Is a SWMS from SiteReady approved or legally checked?

No. SiteReady takes the admin out of writing a SWMS. Your business checks each one against the site, changes it where needed, consults the workers and approves it, as work health and safety law requires. See the terms of use.

Does it use AI?

No. SiteReady works through the WHS laws and codes of practice for the state the project is in, and builds your SWMS from them. We check for changes to the legislation and codes of practice every day. The same task always gives the same draft, and each SWMS lists the laws it is based on.

Which states does it cover?

All states and territories.

What happens to my SWMS if I stop paying?

They stay saved. You can still preview them, and download them again once you re-subscribe. You can't save or download new SWMS until you re-subscribe.

Where is my data kept?

In a database run by our hosting provider. It is never sold or used for advertising. See the privacy policy.

Verify a SWMS

Every SWMS prepared with SiteReady has a reference in its footer, such as SR-ABCD-2345. Enter it to check the SWMS is genuine, see the business it was prepared for, and whether its revision is the current one.

A genuine reference shows the SWMS was prepared with SiteReady for the business named. That business reviews, approves and is responsible for its SWMS. Check the business named here matches the one named on the SWMS. The signed record is the one kept in SiteReady: changes made to a printed or Word copy outside SiteReady are not part of it.

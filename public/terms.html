2. What SiteReady does

SiteReady takes the admin out of writing safe work method statements (SWMS). It turns the task you describe, and the answers you give, into a draft SWMS laid out in the usual format, with job steps, hazards, controls and references to the legislation and codes of practice it used.

SiteReady produces drafts from a rules library we maintain, taken from legislation and codes of practice. It does not use artificial intelligence. The output is generated automatically and may contain errors or omissions.

SiteReady prepares drafts only. It does not approve, certify or sign a SWMS, and a draft from SiteReady is not an approved or legally checked SWMS. It does not tell you whether a SWMS is required for a particular task, and it does not replace a competent person, a safety adviser or legal advice.

SiteReady prepares drafts for every Australian state and territory, using each one's list of high risk construction work and fall height. Drafts for every state and territory cite the section of that state's regulation next to controls taken from the law, and Queensland drafts cite the Queensland approved codes of practice. Victoria's occupational health and safety law is not the model Work Health and Safety Act, so Victorian citations are to the Victorian regulations only, and the wording of some controls follows the model laws. If you work under a different safety regime, the references may not apply.

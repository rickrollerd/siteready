What SiteReady does

SiteReady takes the admin out of writing safe work method statements (SWMS). It turns the task you describe, and the answers you give, into a draft SWMS laid out in the usual format, with job steps, hazards, controls and references to the legislation and codes of practice it used.

SiteReady prepares drafts. It does not approve, certify or sign a SWMS, and a draft from SiteReady is not an approved or legally checked SWMS.

Your responsibility

The business that does the work stays responsible for its SWMS, as it is under work health and safety law. Before a SWMS from SiteReady is used, a competent person in your business must:

  • check it against the actual site, task, plant, people and conditions
  • change, add or remove anything that does not fit
  • consult the workers who will do the work, and the principal contractor where there is one
  • approve it, and make sure workers understand and sign it

Before each download, SiteReady asks you to confirm that you will do this, and records your name on the SWMS.

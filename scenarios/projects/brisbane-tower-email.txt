SiteReady: SWMS for review
50 storey tower, Brisbane CBD: structure (Queensland)

This is the first human test of SiteReady. Seven safe work method statements are attached as Word files, ready to review, change and sign:

01 Floor load-out, tower crane lifts and forklifts
02 Slab formwork, falsework, stripping and backpropping
03 Reo fixing and post-tensioning tendon placement
04 Concrete pumping, placing and finishing
05 Post-tensioning stressing and grouting
06 Core jumpform climbing and core wall construction
07 Precast column and blade wall installation

Each one has: the high risk construction work under section 291 of the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011 (Qld), the key controls by hierarchy, job steps with hazards and controls, PPE tick boxes, responsibilities, a Prepared by section, a principal contractor review section, and worker sign-on pages.

Please note:
- These are drafts. They are not approved or signed.
- Drawing numbers, crane capacities and procedures in them are example inputs for the test. On a real project they come from the project's own designs and procedures.
- Blank lines are left for names, dates, the principal contractor, the Who column, worker consultation and the site-specific section.
- Every step, hazard, control and PPE tick can be changed in Word.

What to look for when reviewing:
1. Is anything wrong, unsafe or misleading?
2. Is anything missing that a principal contractor would reject the SWMS for?
3. Is the wording clear enough for workers on site?
4. Would you use this layout, or change it?

Please reply with your notes, or mark up the Word files and send them back.

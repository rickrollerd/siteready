INTRODUCTION AND OVERVIEW:
The project, Central Facilities is located on the site and will be the central hub for a two hundred individual cabins, beach resort.

Central facilities consist of five separate buildings numbered one to five:

· Building 1 consists of the pool café/bar, storage room and the multi purpose rooms.
· Building 2 consists of the port-cochere, reception, foyer, restaurant, main kitchen, conference centre and admin offices.
· Building 3 is the day spa and gym.
· Building 4 is housekeeping and MDB and Comms room.
· Building 5 is the pool plantroom and tenant laundry.

Buildings 1, 2 and 3 will be made up of:
· Slabs on ground in a raft design
· Structural steel frame with composite purlins
· Aluminium framed glazing and doors
· Aluminium roofing

Building 4 and 5 will be made up of:
· Slabs on ground
· Blockwork
· Timber frames and trusses
· Metal roof

The external works include a 1,000m2 swimming pool and spa. Extensive landscaping, external dining area, DOSA, bar, carpark, loading dock and water features. During the project the adjoining access road will also be developed.

INTRODUCTION AND OVERVIEW:
The project, Central Facilities is located on the site and will be the central hub for a two hundred, individual cabin, beach resort.

Central facilities consist of five separate buildings numbered one to five:

· Building 1 holds the pool café and bar, two multi purpose rooms and kitchen.
· Building 2 consist of the port-cochere, reception, foyer, restaurant, main kitchen, conference centre and admin offices.
· Building 3 is the day spa and gym.
· Building 4 is housekeeping.
· Building 5 is the pool plantroom.

Central facilities also has a 1000m2 swimming pool and spa.
Buildings 1, 2 and 3 will be made up of:
· Slabs on ground in a raft design
· Structural steel frame with composite purlins
· Aluminium framed glazing and doors
· Aluminium roofing

Building 4 and 5 will be made up of:
· Slabs on ground
· Blockwork
· Steel frames and timber trusses
· Metal roof

DESCRIPTION OF WORK AREA:
The scope of work includes but is not limited to the following principal areas: -
· Provide and install ALL Vinyl and Carpet throughout the entire Central Facilities as per the Scope Mark Up.

SPECIFIC INCLUSIONS
1. Installation of Vinyl to Buildings 2B, 2A & Building 1. Allowing for heat weld to all joins and coving 100mm to ALL walls as specified in tender documentation.
2. Supply, order and install custom Hospitality Carpet (specified supplier). The direct stick method will be applied for the installation too the subfloor. This is limited to the areas specified as CA2/3 on the Scope Mark Up.
3. Supply and Install the specified commercial carpet tile to Building 2B as specified on the tender documentation, this is to be installed using the direct stick method.
4. Preparation to skim subfloor 1mm to sheet Vinyl areas.
5. The Subcontractor has allowed for ALL materials needed for the installation of both Vinyl and Carpet. This includes Coving, epoxy adhesive, trims and spare materials for maintenance.

1.0 Works Background

Introduction and Overview

This scope of works involves the design of temporary works, design certification, safety, supervision, manufacture, transportation to site, installation, plant and materials necessary to complete the WATERPROOFING – SUPPLY AND INSTALL in its entirety to the extent indicated on the drawings and in accordance with the Subcontract, specifications, Australian standards, industry codes of practice, to the satisfaction of all authorities and related items which form part of the project. 

The project is a mixed use retail, commercial and residential development situated between three street frontages. 

The Residential Tower is to achieve a 4 Star Green Star Design and As Built Rating. The Commercial Tower is to achieve a 5 Star Design and As Built rating.

The Sub Contractor is to allow to supply and install the waterproofing to meet the requirements of the respective credits sought relative to the sub contractors’ scope.  The Green Star matrices identifying the targets is included in the specification section of the contract documents.

The commercial office building is along one street frontage. It is 17,500m2 and is made up of 11 levels plus a plant room. 

The residential building is made up of 234 units. There are 21 levels plus a plant room. The ground floor level will be used for retail.  

The mixed use building on the secondary street frontage is made up of 4 floors.

There are 2 full plan area basements and a 3rd basement that covers approximately half of the site.



Description of Work Area

Except as may be expressly excluded in the Subcontract, the Subcontractor shall provide all labour, materials, plant and equipment necessary to supply, install and certify the waterproofing as detailed below required for a complete and functioning building, fit for its purpose, including but not limited to:

The scope of work includes but is not limited to the following principal areas: 

1. The Subcontractor has allowed to supply and install waterproofing as indicated on the marked up drawings attached to this Subcontract (Document No. LD316 to LD321 contained in Schedule 9) and in accordance with the “Waterproofing Product – Location and Brand” table as contained in the specification.

2. The Subcontractor is responsible for all surface preparation, primers and subsequent coatings to provide a complete waterproof system to all nominated areas.  This includes for filling / grinding all blow holes, pin holes, recesses, chips, slurry, concrete dags, etc to ensure that the substrate is suitable to accept the membrane as per the project specifications, manufacturers recommendations and relevant standards and codes. 

3. Allow for all floor membranes to turn up walls a minimum 150mm (UNO).  Where there is no wall finish to be applied, allow to tape the wall to provide a neat and straight finished membrane line.   

4. For all plinths to have waterproof membrane applied allow for installing the waterproof membrane under the plinth prior to casting. Once the plinth is cast, the waterproof membrane should then be applied over the plinth.

5. For all services penetrations in concrete roofs allow to seal around the services penetrating the roof to ensure it is water tight.  Over flashings are to be by the relevant services contractor.

6. The Subcontractor is responsible for return visits to complete waterproofing to penetrations as may be required by the Contractor.  This will include, but is not limited to the Tower Crane penetrations and hoist infill slabs.  

7. Allow for sand seeding all membranes as per the manufacturers’ recommendations where the membrane is to be covered with a tile bed or a tile.

8. The commencement of the Works will be taken to mean acceptance of such surfaces as being satisfactory and defects in the Works resulting from accepting poor surfaces are to be corrected by the Subcontractor at no cost to the Contractor.

9. The Subcontractor is to meet and provide the relevant documentation to ensure compliance with the relevant Green Star (both design and as built) requirements.

10. The Subcontractor is responsible for protection of all works and to make good any damages to the Works.  The Subcontractor is to remove protection when directed by the Contractor.

11. Accept responsibility for and protect all adjacent surfaces from damage and disfigurement that may occur as a result of the Subcontract Works.  Replace all damaged materials and protect finished surfaces to the satisfaction of the Contractor. 

12. Protect adjoining properties from any damage whatsoever. Rectification of any damage to the building or to adjoining properties and trees including the making good to work disturbed as a result of the Subcontract Works.

13. The Subcontractor is to ensure that the waterproofing system is compatible with all substrates, sealants, sealers, paints, patching material, curing compounds, etc that the waterproof system will be in contact with.

14. Strict compliance with all the Contractors Quality Assurance requirements including completion of Inspection Test Plans (ITPs), associated check lists, compliance with “the builder's Waterproofing and Tiling Manual” (refer LD260) and relevant Australian Standards. 

15. The Subcontractor is to allow to carry out and document all substrate and waterproof membrane system testing as required by the specifications and manufacturer’s requirements to confirm the substrate is suitable to accept the waterproof membrane system and that the system has been applied in accordance with the manufacturer’s recommendations.

16. Allow for the protection and/or masking of floors, fittings and similar work.

17. Submit colour / texture samples (min 300mm x 300mm) for approval of the relevant waterproofing membranes.

18. Carry out sample sections on Site as requested by the Contractor and prior to commencement of waterproofing.

19. Provide suitable screening while sanding/grinding to prevent dust spreading to other areas and out from the building to the open air.

20. Ensure that any exposed metal items in concrete are cut off, ground back, epoxy coated and filled by prior to patching, skimming/rendering and waterproofing.

21. Provide any extraction fans or ventilation required for fumes, glues and similar items.

22. Provision, erection and removal of all scaffold and access platforms, including swinging stages. Licensed scaffolders to be engaged where required for erection, dismantling and alterations. No scaffolding is to be altered in any way by any unqualified persons (eg. movement of hop-up platforms, planks, corner stage brackets, etc.)

23. Precautions must be taken to ensure that there is not health or safety risk to others when toxic or volatile elements are used (eg. two-packs, enamels, polyurethanes, etc).  Any such substances shall comply with all statute and OHS Regulations in this regard. Where required, the Subcontractor will provide adequate signage, barricading, containment and/or mechanical ventilation in the work area and ensure that fumes and overspray are not exhausted into an area where members of the public or workers may be at risk from airborne concentrations of the substance.

24. Allow for attending upon and making good after all other trades, cutting out and making good after all other trades, cutting out and making good defective work and for cleaning off and leaving the Works perfect on completion.

25. Install waterproofing membranes to the areas including for trimming into/up to and around all pipes, collars, outlets and the like.

26. Provide all membranes to up stands, hobs, etc required to provide continuous water barrier throughout all waterproofed areas.

27. Provide all cover flashings as required including caulking to same on completion.

28. Install F.C. linings or similar to sides and bases of all planters to provide adequate protection to the membrane.

29. Water test for minimum of 12 hours all waterproofing to internal areas or external areas over habitable spaces. Records to be kept of water testing and countersigned by the Contractor and provided on completion as part of the Subcontractor’s quality assurance.  Where water testing is not possible allow to carry out “Holiday” testing to the waterproof membrane.

30. For all membranes not covered by tiles or other finishes allow to carry out slip tests to each of the products in accordance with the relevant Australia Standards / Codes and by a Nata certified testing authority.

31. Attendance during concreting or backfilling operations and making good to any damage that may occur.

32. Drying out as required of all surfaces to receive membrane.  Include provision of primer as required to compensate for concrete dampness to facilitate bonding of the membrane and in accordance with the manufacturers’ recommendation. This also to be allowed to maintain the contractors programme requirements.

33. Ensure that only membranes and systems that have been approved for use by the Contractor are used.

34. Ensure that the waterproofing/tanking is compatible with all sealants and glues that are being adhered to the waterproofing.

35. Where there is a potential for waterproofing solvents to be confined during the curing of waterproofing, the Subcontractor is to provide adequate means to disperse solvents.

36. Attend Site and apply membranes to suit the Contractor’s programme requirements.  Allow to complete areas in a staged manner and at different times during the project.  Allow for reactivation of membranes as per the manufacturers’ recommendation.

37. Wast out / cleaning of material / equipment in designated areas only.

38. Provide 100 x 100mm angle bunds to the basement plenum doors.

39. Allow for an epoxy primer to all areas to enable membrane application sooner.

40. Allow for waterproofing to all glazing reveals including the balcony door heads, sills and reveals.

41. Allow for slip testing of the trafficable areas where waterproof membrane is applied. 

42. Allow to supply, install and remove as required of the temporary waterproofing works.

43. Allow to supply and install roof membrane system to residential building which consists of Geofabric top and bottom of the insulation, liquid-applied membrane (zero VOC) with 50mm zero ODP insulation board and washed ballast. The drainage gravel shall be 25-45mm in size with at least 75mm deep. 

44. Allow to cut reglet to roof level precast so that membrane and flashing can be securely fixed into the reglet.

45. Allow for waterproofing to Residential building windows where it meets partition walls as indicated on the marked up drawings attached to this Subcontract (Document No. LD372 contained in Schedule 9).
